Food-name matches and nearby results

A dish listed by an editor, a name or description match, and a related cuisine are different kinds of results. Related cuisine is a suggestion to ask the venue, not confirmation that it serves your requested dish. Menus, availability and opening hours can change. Distances are straight-line estimates from your shared position or the selected town center, not travel distances. Public map data is incomplete and result limits apply; the first result is only the closest among matching available records, not a guarantee of the closest venue in the world. Confirm directly before travelling. Public search providers can fail or impose limits; Google Maps links open a separate external service.
