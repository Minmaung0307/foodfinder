FoodFinder search fixes — 2026-10-13

Root causes found in the supplied source:
- Search only filtered the previously loaded nearby collection.
- Nearby queries excluded supermarkets, delis and other food shops.
- Udon and Pho had no dish/cuisine vocabulary.
- Nearby and targeted searches shared a query-independent cache.
- Radius changes erased the query; retry reused fresh cache.
- Zero matches silently showed unrelated places.
- Broad area results are capped at 2,000 records, not necessarily nearest first.

Changes:
Search now requests query-specific mapped results in the selected radius and merges them with existing local results. Query-specific caches use a new version. Retry forces a request. Radius changes preserve the query. Food shops, markets, bars and pubs are included. Udon, ramen and pho have related-cuisine suggestions explicitly labeled as unconfirmed. Alternative names and brands are searchable; NB96 / NB 96 spacing is tolerated. Direct matches precede related suggestions. No matches remain an honest empty state with a Google Maps search link. Provider failure and stale-cache messages remain visible.
